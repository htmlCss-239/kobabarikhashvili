The sky darkened quickly as storm clouds rolled in over the hills, casting long shadows across the quiet valley. Birds that had been chirping moments before fell silent, sensing the change in the air. A low rumble of thunder echoed in the distance, growing louder with each passing minute. Trees swayed gently at first, then more violently as the wind picked up speed. A single drop of rain splashed onto the dusty road, followed by a sudden downpour that soaked the earth within seconds. People rushed for cover, their footsteps muffled by the pounding rain. The once-still village came alive with movement, umbrellas opening like flowers blooming in reverse.

Inside a small cottage near the edge of town, a girl sat by the window, her eyes wide with wonder. She pressed her hand to the cool glass, tracing the path of raindrops as they raced down the pane. Lightning lit up the room for a brief second, revealing shelves lined with books and half-finished sketches. Despite the storm outside, a sense of calm filled the room, broken only by the occasional crack of thunder. Her cat curled beside her, purring softly as if unfazed by the chaos. With a soft smile, she turned back to her notebook, the storm becoming nothing more than background music to her imagination.
